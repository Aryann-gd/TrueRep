From joint angle kinematics to instant barcode macro scans, TrueRep delivers an uncompromised offline experience.

33-Point MediaPipe Pose Engine

Tracks 33 spatial landmarks at 30 FPS using hardware-accelerated CameraX. Vector dot-products compute precise biomechanical angles across shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, and ankles.

  • Dynamic EMA landmark smoothing eliminates jitter
  • Accurate elbow & knee joint angle degrees
  • Real-time spine alignment posture monitoring

Deterministic 3-State FSM

Eliminates false counts with hysteresis guards. A repetition only increments when you progress through START (>160°), sustain INFLECTION (≤90° for ≥100ms), and achieve full COMPLETION.

  • Rejects half-reps and spastic bounces
  • Hysteresis buffers prevent threshold fluttering
  • Full cadence and eccentric/concentric tempo tracking

Real-Time Spoken Voice Coach

Workout without looking at the screen. A prioritized Text-to-Speech audio queue delivers immediate spoken corrections like "Chest to floor" or "Keep your hips level" before injury happens.

  • Priority 1 safety overrides non-critical messages
  • Anti-spam throttling suppresses repeat cues
  • Audio ducking blends coaching smoothly with workout music

Calisthenics Movement Library

Equipped with pre-configured movement profiles for Push-ups, Squats, Planks, Lunges, Bicep Curls, and Glute Bridges. Includes animated form demos and kinematic target envelopes.

  • Pre-calibrated angle envelopes per movement
  • Isometric hold cycle engine for planks & static holds
  • Offline JSON movement database

Smart Nutrition & Barcode Scanner

Point your camera at food packages or dishes. Instant barcode scanning matches against the Open Food Facts database to log calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats in seconds.

  • Instant offline food cache & barcode recognition
  • Macro goal breakdown (Protein / Carbs / Fats)
  • Zero manual typing or guesswork

Streak Shields & Gamification

Build unbreakable fitness momentum. Earn XP for every valid repetition, level up your athlete tier, unlock cosmetic avatar profile frames, and arm Streak Freeze Shields for rest days.

  • Streak Freeze Shields protect planned rest days
  • Opt-in rewarded ad streak repair mechanics
  • Cosmetic frame shop with in-app coin balances
PRIVACY-FIRST ENGINEERING

Your Reps Stay on Your Phone.
No Cloud Video. No Exceptions.

We believe your living room, workout garage, and biometric metrics belong to you. TrueRep processes camera frames exclusively in volatile RAM and discards them immediately.

Zero Cloud Streaming

Camera frames never leave device memory. No video chunks or coordinates are transmitted to remote servers.

No Mandatory Accounts

Launch the app and start training immediately. No forced email registrations, passwords, or social tracking logins.

Android Health Connect

Sync your daily steps, body weight, and workout calories directly with Google's on-device Health Connect platform.
